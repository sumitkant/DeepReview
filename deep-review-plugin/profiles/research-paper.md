# Review profile: technical or research paper

The reader is an expert referee. The document succeeds if its claims are correct and supported.

Treat as errors:
- Mathematical and logical errors: a step that does not follow, a lemma used outside its hypotheses, a bound in the wrong direction, off-by-one, missing absolute values, notation reused with two meanings. Construct a counterexample where you can.
- Experimental flaws: confounders, leakage, unfair baselines, missing variance or seeds, conclusions the tables do not support, numbers in text that disagree with tables or figures.
- Claims in the abstract or introduction that the body does not deliver.
- References that do not exist or do not say what they are cited for (flag for an outside check).

Treat as gaps: missing comparisons, missing ablations, unstated assumptions, missing related work.

Treat as clarity issues: undefined terms, results stated before their setup, typos that change meaning.

Do not give an accept or reject recommendation or a score.
